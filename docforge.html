Project 01 · AI-Native Document OS

DocForge AI

An AI-native document authoring platform that manages the complete document lifecycle—from planning and writing to formatting, PDF generation, and final export.

View on GitHub ↗
Description

DocForge AI is an AI-native document authoring platform that replaces the traditional workflow of using a chatbot alongside Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

Instead of simply assisting with writing, the AI acts as the primary author, generating, editing, formatting, and refining professional documents (such as reports, research papers, resumes, proposals, and legal documents) from start to finish.

What Makes It Different

Most AI writing tools treat AI as a chatbot attached to a document. DocForge AI treats AI as the document author.

Instead of users constantly prompting, copying, and pasting between chat and an editor, the AI manages the complete document lifecycle—creating a seamless, uninterrupted authoring experience.

Core Features
  • AI-First Document Creation: Designed natively for reports, research papers, resumes, proposals, and legal documents.
  • Context-Aware Writing (RAG): Retrieval-Augmented Generation over uploaded documents ensures factually grounded and highly contextual drafts.
  • Live PDF Preview: Instantly updates the visual output as the document is generated.
  • Automatic Continuation: When the LLM reaches its token limit, it auto-continues seamlessly, so users never have to press "Continue".
  • Advanced AI Capabilities: Built-in tools for editing, rewriting, formatting, citations, tables, and document restructuring.
Long-Term Vision

The platform currently uses powerful open-source foundation models like Qwen and Gemma.

Moving forward, it will evolve into a specialized document authoring model through fine-tuning on high-quality document datasets and anonymized user-approved edits, continuously improving its mastery over formatting, tone, and professional structures.
